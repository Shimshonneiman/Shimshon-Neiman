CONFIDENTIALITY BY DESIGN

The client keeps their story. The therapist keeps the boundary.

Clients choose whether to request funding and what, if anything, to share publicly. Therapists verify professional facts but do not write appeals. Donors never receive clinical notes.

✓ No donor account required to browse
✓ Anonymous and private client request options in the design
✓ Professional verification before funding
✓ Clinical records are kept separate from donor information
